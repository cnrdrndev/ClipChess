# ClipChess ♟️✨

ClipChess is a modern, open-source chess platform inspired by Lichess. It allows chess players to play against computer bots or friends, analyze games with a real Stockfish engine, chat with other users, and share brilliant game clips and PGNs with the community.

## Features
- **Play Modes**: Play against configurable Stockfish AI bots or local 2D friend matches.
- **Move Navigation**: Click through any previous move in the history table to inspect past board positions.
- **Real Stockfish Integration**: Powered by Stockfish running via Web Workers for real-time engine evaluation and bot moves.
- **Community Clips & PGN Sharing**: Share your tactical masterpieces to the global community feed with interactive miniature board previews.
- **User Accounts & ELO**: Persistent local accounts with dynamic rating tracking.
- **Direct Messaging**: Chat and coordinate with other players on the platform.

## Tech Stack
- **HTML5 & CSS3** (Custom Lichess-inspired dark mode UI)
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**
- **Chess.js** (Move validation and PGN handling)
- **Stockfish.js** (Chess engine analysis)
- **FontAwesome** (Icons)
